## Cinematic engineering model
The current 12.3-second opening uses locally authored Three.js geometry inspired by `rgi-action-2.jpg` and `rgi-action-6.jpg`: sheet-metal chassis, scoops, rubber wheels, nylon hubs, DC motors, taped battery and exposed electronics. It is illustrative, not a scan or exact replica. The 250 instanced spectators and confetti react to the goal. The existing CGI component cutouts and native accessory geometry scatter across the viewport. The actual scored football mesh moves into a small transparent foreground renderer, preserving its world-space pose, and flies into the O in GOAL above descending navy curtains. Nine contiguous clipped slices assemble the existing dark-theme wordmark, which then disperses before the curtain opens. Cyan/gold sweeps, speckles and gold trim provide the party theme. No new video, generated image or third-party model is used. Shaders and local component artwork prepare before playback. Stadium rendering stops once covered; the two-mesh ball layer stops after its wordmark handoff. Resources are disposed on completion. First-paint gating and reduced-motion/no-JavaScript fallbacks remain. Preparation is capped at 3.5 seconds, playback at 16 seconds, with a 22-second bootstrap escape.
