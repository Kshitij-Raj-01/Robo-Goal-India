## Cinematic engineering model
The opening uses locally authored Three.js geometry inspired by `rgi-action-2.jpg` and `rgi-action-6.jpg`: sheet-metal chassis, scoops, rubber wheels, nylon hubs, DC motors, taped battery and exposed electronics. It is illustrative, not a scan or exact replica. The background adds 250 instanced waving/clapping/jumping spectators and flags; goal scoring intensifies the reaction. Hardware separates into a mix of native meshes and realistic rendered cutouts, including ESP32, Arduino, battery, driver, wheels, motors, gears, servo, ultrasonic sensor, capacitors, MOSFETs, connectors, cables and fasteners. White/light-orange fabric curtains display the supplied logo. The bot emerges from the football O before the homepage reveal. No video asset or third-party model is used. The server-rendered cover, synchronous head gate, reduced-motion/no-JavaScript fallback and bounded timeouts remain. Preparation is capped at 6.5 seconds, playback at 11 seconds, with a 15-second bootstrap escape.
